## Set 21 — Story Structure & Plot Reasoning

### Q5,501. What usually introduces the characters and setting?

A) Beginning
B) Ending
C) Index
D) Glossary

**Answer: A) Beginning**
**Explanation:** A story's beginning often introduces who, where and when.


### Q5,502. A child loses a library book. What is the story's main problem?

A) The child likes blue
B) The weather is warm
C) The book is missing
D) The library has chairs

**Answer: C) The book is missing**
**Explanation:** The missing book creates a problem to solve.


### Q5,503. A girl searches three rooms for her lost scarf. Which part of the plot is this?

A) Table of contents
B) Author biography
C) Book title
D) Attempt to solve the problem

**Answer: D) Attempt to solve the problem**
**Explanation:** Searching is an action taken to solve the problem.


### Q5,504. A boy finds his missing dog at the shelter. Which story part is this?

A) Resolution
B) Unrelated detail
C) Introduction only
