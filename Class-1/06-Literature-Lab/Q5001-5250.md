## Set 10 — Ultimate Literature Olympiad & HOTS

### Q5,226. A story has a beginning where a girl finds a map, a middle where she follows clues, and an ending where she finds a hidden garden. What is the main structure?
A) Beginning–problem/adventure–resolution  
B) Ending–title–dictionary  
C) Question–answer–recipe  
D) Picture–cover–page number

**Answer: A**
**Explanation:** The story introduces the adventure, develops it through clues and ends with a discovery.

### Q5,227. Which detail is strongest evidence that a character is responsible?
A) They finish their task without being reminded.  
B) They wear a red shirt.  
C) They like mangoes.  
D) They have a large backpack.

**Answer: A**
**Explanation:** Completing a task without reminders is direct evidence of responsibility.

### Q5,228. A story's title is 'The Empty Nest'. Which event is most likely relevant?

C) A train crossing a bridge  
D) A chef baking bread

**Answer: A**
**Explanation:** An empty nest strongly suggests birds or young animals have left.

### Q5,229. A character is afraid of speaking in front of the class but practises and succeeds. Which theme is best supported?
A) Practice can build confidence  
B) Never try new things  
C) Fear always wins

**Answer: A**
**Explanation:** The character's practice leads to successful performance.

### Q5,230. Which sentence gives both a setting and an action?
A) In the quiet forest, the fox searched for food.

C) Food was delicious.  
D) The forest was green.

**Explanation:** It tells where the event happens and what the fox does.

### Q5,231. If two characters disagree but listen carefully and find a solution, what skill is demonstrated?
