# SuccessLabs Academy — Enterprise Assessment Ecosystem

**Status:** Governance baseline proposed v0.1; question production not yet started under this blueprint.

**Mission:** Architecting Experiences for a Better World.

**Architecture is the North Star. The 20 Labs drive assessments.** Seven enterprise domains provide context; 12 architecture streams and 11 GICS sectors provide cross-cutting analytical lenses.

**Target:** 20 Labs × 7 domains × 1,000 original challenges = 140,000. This is a target, not a published count.

## Principles and standards
**Principles** are enduring decision rules for professional judgment. **Standards** are explicit requirements that can be tested and audited. Both apply across every Lab and domain.

1. [Enterprise Assessment Principles](./standards/00-Enterprise-Assessment-Principles.md)

**Vendor-agnostic:** SAP may be a replaceable exemplar, never a North Star, product endorsement or source of tactical trivia.

**Initial pilot:** 25 independently reviewed HR Discover challenges before scaling to the first 250-question volume.
