# Enterprise Assessment Principles — v0.1 (Proposed)

A **principle** is enduring guidance for judgment and trade-offs. A **standard** is a specific, auditable requirement. Both govern all 20 Labs, seven domains, 12 architecture streams and 11 GICS sectors.

| ID | Principle | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| P01 | Architecture as North Star | Enterprise coherence and strategic outcomes guide decisions |
| P02 | Business Value First | Start from stakeholders, capabilities, value streams and outcomes |
| P03 | Vendor and Product Neutrality | Technology is a replaceable exemplar, never the answer |
| P04 | Evergreen Knowledge | Prioritize durable concepts over transactions and interface trivia |
| P05 | Critical Thinking Over Recall | Assess reasoning, comparison, synthesis and judgment |
| P06 | Systems Thinking | Consider interdependencies, feedback loops and side effects |
| P07 | Human-Centered and Ethical Design | Protect dignity, accessibility, privacy, fairness and sustainability |
| P08 | Evidence-Based Decisions | Distinguish evidence, assumptions, hypotheses and recommendations |
| P09 | Industry and Context Awareness | Respect sector economics, risks, regulations and constraints |
| P10 | Experiential Learning | Use realistic decisions, experiments, simulations and reflection |
| P11 | Continuous Adaptation | Improve using learner evidence, reviews and feedback |
| P12 | Traceability and Reusability | Link competencies, questions, streams and business outcomes |

When principles conflict, record options, stakeholders, evidence, risks and accountable decision. A standard exception needs documented approval and cannot waive ethical, safety or legal duties. SAP may be used as an illustrative exemplar but not promoted as the North Star.
