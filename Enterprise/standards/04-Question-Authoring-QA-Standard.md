# Question Authoring and QA Standard — v0.1 (Proposed)

**Applies to every Lab-domain pairing.** A standard is auditable; a principle explains why the requirement matters.

## Required fields
ID; domain; Lab; learning objective; primary architecture stream; optional secondary streams; GICS sector or cross-industry; proficiency; skills; scenario; question type; prompt; options or scoring rubric; correct answer or exemplar; rationale; alternatives and trade-offs; evidence/source notes; author; reviewer; review status; version; last-reviewed date.

## Standards
- **S01 Stable identity:** unique question ID, e.g. HR-DESIGN-0001; never reuse for a different item.
- **S02 Outcome:** explicit business value or learning objective.
- **S03 Architecture:** one primary stream and an explained architectural principle.
- **S04 Vendor neutrality:** no product navigation, transaction code, vendor feature recall or version trivia; SAP is only a replaceable exemplar.
- **S05 Reasoning:** beyond Foundation, require analysis, trade-offs, interpretation or judgment.
- **S06 Accuracy:** validate claims and note jurisdictional or time-sensitive assumptions.
- **S07 Originality:** check exact and semantic duplicates; no template shuffling to inflate counts.
- **S08 Accessibility:** clear language, fair assumptions and inclusive scenarios.
- **S09 Scoring:** defensible keys for MCQs; explicit rubrics and acceptable alternatives for open responses.
- **S10 Provenance:** retain sources, authorship, review evidence and revision history.
- **S11 Publication gates:** schema, numbering, answer alignment, originality and editorial checks must pass.
- **S12 Honest reporting:** planned, drafted, reviewed, validated and published counts must remain distinct.

## Review
Lab owner drafts → domain reviewer checks realism → architecture reviewer checks principles and alternatives → editor checks assessment quality → publisher verifies automated checks → learner feedback informs revisions. A standards exception needs an owner, rationale, expiry, compensating controls and approval; principles still apply.

## Example
**HR-DESIGN-0001:** Multiple plants need limited workforce attributes. Which approach balances consistency, least privilege and resilience? **Best answer:** Governed information model, accountable data ownership, scoped integration contracts and monitored recovery. **Why:** Addresses stewardship, semantics, privacy and operational continuity without assuming a particular product.
