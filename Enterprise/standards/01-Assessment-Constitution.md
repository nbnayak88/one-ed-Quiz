# Enterprise Assessment Constitution — v0.1 (Proposed)

**Mission:** Architecting Experiences for a Better World.

**North Star:** Architecture. **Engines:** 20 experiential Labs. **Contexts:** seven domains (HR, CX, Finance, SCM, Procurement, BTM, Leadership & Identity Transformation). **Lenses:** 12 architecture streams and 11 GICS sectors.

**Planning target, not published inventory:** 20 Labs × 7 domains × 1,000 original challenges = 140,000 questions. Four 250-question volumes per Lab-domain pairing = 560 volumes.

## Foundations
- TOGAF: architecture principles, governance, requirements, baseline/target, roadmaps and transitions.
- Business Architecture Guild / BIZBOK: capabilities, value streams, stakeholders, information, strategy and initiatives.
- DAMA-DMBOK: data governance, quality, stewardship, metadata, master data and lifecycle.
- Business analysis: problem definition, elicitation, requirements, traceability, option assessment and solution evaluation.
- Consulting reasoning: hypothesis-driven inquiry, structured decomposition, evidence synthesis and defensible recommendations.
- Future-work capabilities: analytical and creative thinking, resilience, collaboration, empathy, technological literacy and responsible judgment.

These are principle-level alignments, not endorsements or reproductions of proprietary framework material.

## Governance
Every question starts with a business need or architectural decision. Vendor names and product-specific commands cannot determine correctness. SAP can appear as a replaceable exemplar, not as an endorsed destination. Distinguish facts from assumptions and show the rationale for decisions.

**Roles:** Lab owner defines competency; domain reviewer validates business realism; architecture reviewer evaluates principles and trade-offs; assessment editor checks clarity, accessibility and scoring; publisher verifies structure and provenance.

**Lifecycle:** planned → drafted → reviewed → validated → published → monitored → retired. Publication does not automatically imply certification readiness.
